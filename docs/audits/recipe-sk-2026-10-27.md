## Live data and general fixes

42 deployed S/K API requests returned 701 sampled regression products. The responses retain their original source and price units, including both Ruoanhinta cents and S-kaupat euro responses. Makaronilaatikko and Bataatti-porkkanakeitto supplied 34 S/K checks of identifiable ingredient requirements, all with compatible candidates. The generic source line “rasvaa” was not declared an exact product identity. Munakas and Tomaatti-kalakeitto were also inspected: vague filling, fish and herb lines remain reviewable, with unknown quantities preserved.

The new corpus rejects 70 real wrong-type candidates. General corrections cover English cracker/snack names under spices; raw minced meat versus seasoning/burgers/prepared meals; whole eggs versus substitutes/whites/pasta; kitchen accessory metadata; plain pepper versus flavoured meals and mixes; and multi-vegetable packs named without “mix”. Butter described as “nokare” and inflected shrimp now retain usable food identities without inventing quantities.

Missing recipe ingredients wait for explicit skip and are then retained as unpriced memo rows in the cart. Unspecified or count-only loose weights cannot be added as if a kilogram quote were a known item price; their weight review remains visible and automatic refresh cannot fill that unsafe price.

## Checks and limits

The new S/K regression executes the actual desktop selection and missing-step functions at their UI/provider boundaries. Existing 20-recipe, diverse-product, protein-state, query-coverage, online-transfer, retail-quantity and K-category tests passed, along with strict TypeScript checks for the recipe modules and TSX syntax checks. The old desktop-comparison-substitution-test.cjs is stale against the current branch (it expects a removed desktopStrictCompareIdentity helper); it is not claimed as passing. Live browser comparison evidence is recorded above.

This round distinguishes the real browser chains from API-corpus checks. It does not claim every vague wiki ingredient has an automatic exact product or that stock/prices are identical across stores.
